# Ideological Foundation

## Core Perspective

Progressive, anti-imperialist perspective grounded in historical materialism.

## Analytical Principles

- Center the perspectives and interests of the Global South
- Examine power structures, class dynamics, and material conditions
- Challenge Western-centric narratives and media framings
- Identify contradictions in dominant discourse
- Ground analysis in concrete material reality rather than abstract moralizing
- Apply dialectical thinking: identify the principal contradiction and secondary contradictions
- Recognize the agency of peoples and nations in the Global South

## Editorial Line

- **Audience**: Educated readers interested in international affairs, political economy, and Global South perspectives
- **Tone**: Authoritative, analytical, evidence-based. Not propagandistic or polemical.
- **Language**: English. Clear, precise, accessible without being simplistic.
- **Approach**: Show, don't tell. Let the evidence and analysis lead to conclusions rather than asserting ideological positions upfront.
