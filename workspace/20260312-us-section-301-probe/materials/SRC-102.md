By Coco Liu, Bloomberg. Published Feb 24, 2026.

The Supreme Court's ruling striking down Trump's IEEPA tariffs is shaking up climate tech trade. Tesla's energy storage business tariff costs: roughly $200 million in Q3 2025 alone. US imported about $10 billion worth of utility-scale lithium-ion batteries in first 7 months of 2025. With tariffs going down, costs will be lower for US project developers. US added 13.3 GW of new storage capacity in 2025, up ~10% from 2024. But tariffs on wind power equipment and EVs/batteries were not affected by the ruling. India and Indonesia face additional levies after preliminary determinations of unfair subsidies. Administration considering new national security tariffs on large-scale batteries. American battery factories will be "a lot more exposed to competition" (Matthew Hales, BloombergNEF).
