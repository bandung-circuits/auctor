# Research Overview

## Research Topic

AI-powered news commentary production: from a single news lead to a comprehensive analytical commentary article.

## Core Research Questions

For each news event, the system investigates:

1. **What happened?** — Complete event facts, timeline, key actors, quantitative data
2. **Why did it happen?** — Historical context, policy background, structural causes, theoretical frameworks
3. **How are different actors responding?** — Government positions, media framings, expert analyses
4. **What does it mean?** — Geopolitical implications, power dynamics, impact on Global South

## Research Scope

- **Includes**: International affairs, political economy, geopolitics, Global South perspectives, historical context, academic analysis
- **Excludes**: Domestic tabloid news, celebrity gossip, sports results, purely technical product reviews

## Research Stance

As defined in `references/domain/ideological-foundation.md`:
- Progressive, anti-imperialist, grounded in historical materialism
- Center Global South perspectives
- Challenge Western-centric narratives with evidence

## Ten Research Angles

Research is organized into 10 angles across 4 layers:

### Factual Layer (事实)

| Angle | Excerpt Prefix | Description |
|-------|-----------|-------------|
| Event Overview | M-A | Complete event narrative: 5W1H, timeline, key actors, core facts |
| Statistical Data | M-B | Quantitative data, economic indicators, trend statistics; cross-verify sources |

### Context Layer (背景)

| Angle | Excerpt Prefix | Description |
|-------|-----------|-------------|
| Historical Context | M-C | Historical origins, policy evolution, long-term trends, analogous events |
| Domain Knowledge | M-D | Technical knowledge, industry context, legal frameworks, economic mechanisms, international conventions |
| Academic Research | M-E | Academic literature, theoretical debates, research findings relevant to the event |

### Reaction Layer (反应)

| Angle | Excerpt Prefix | Description |
|-------|-----------|-------------|
| Government Positions | M-F | Official positions, policy responses, diplomatic statements from relevant governments |
| Media Coverage | M-G | Reporting angle differences and narrative comparisons across media with different stances |
| Expert Commentary | M-H | Think tank assessments, industry expert views, independent analyst insights |

### Analysis Layer (分析)

| Angle | Excerpt Prefix | Description |
|-------|-----------|-------------|
| Geopolitics | M-I | Power structures, interstate dynamics, international organization roles, Global South connections |
| Impact & Outlook | M-J | Short/long-term consequences, industry impacts, Global South implications, possible trajectories |

## Input Parameters

- `{NEWS_LEAD}`: A one-sentence description or URL of the news event to analyze
