By Ben Walpole | 3/26/2025

Overall grade: C (highest since 1998, up from C- in 2021). Nine of 18 categories earned D or D+ grades.

$9.1 trillion investment required to bring all infrastructure to good repair. IIJA $1.2 trillion only covered part of needs. IIJA funding expires 2026.

12.7 million American households lack broadband. "We are coming back from years of no investment, years of deterioration."
