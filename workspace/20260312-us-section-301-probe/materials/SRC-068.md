# [SRC-068] China Commerce Ministry Official Statement on Section 301 Investigation (Feb 25, 2026)

**Source URL**: http://www.mofcom.gov.cn/syxwfb/art/2026/art_ba8c5048c3ed45078d08cd88f2e95879.html
**Fetch Time**: 2026-03-12T20:45:00Z
**Source Type**: Official Government Sources (China Ministry of Commerce)
**Credibility**: High

**Original Content**:

商务部新闻发言人就美国贸易代表格里尔针对中国履行中美第一阶段经贸协议情况301调查相关言论答记者问

答：中美第一阶段经贸协议2020年初生效后，中方秉持契约精神，认真履行协议义务，在加强知识产权保护、推动金融和农产品市场开放等方面已如期完成协议承诺。反观美方，对华加严出口管制、限制双向投资，持续升级打压限制措施，违反协议精神，破坏协议执行的氛围与条件。

中方希望美方客观、理性看待第一阶段协议的实施问题，不要"甩锅推责"，更不要借机"生事""挑事"。如果美方执意推进相关调查，甚至以调查为由出台关税等限制性措施，中方将采取一切必要措施，坚决捍卫自身合法权益。

[Translation: China upheld the spirit of the Phase One agreement, fulfilling its obligations on IP protection and market opening. In contrast, the U.S. tightened export controls, restricted investment, and escalated suppressive measures. China hopes the U.S. will not "shift blame" or "provoke trouble." If the U.S. insists on advancing the investigation or uses it to impose tariffs, China will take all necessary measures to firmly defend its legitimate rights and interests.]
