  Common_Misconceptions_Among_Western_Marxists:
    causes_of_misreadings:
      - Layer_1_Epistemic_Foundations:
          - Idealist_vs_Materialist_Analysis: >
              Some reduce SWCC to a deviation from "pure" socialism, prioritizing doctrinal
              ideals (e.g., "markets = capitalism") over China's historical-material conditions
              (post-colonial poverty, agrarian backwardness, imperialist encirclement).
          - Ahistorical_Universals: >
              Framing socialism as a static blueprint rather than a process adapting to concrete
              contradictions (e.g., Deng's reforms as a strategic retreat to build productive forces,
              per Marxist stages theory).

      - Layer_2_Productive_Forces_and_Class_Relations:
          - Misreading_the_Socialist_Market: >
              ignoring the CPC's retention
              of strategic command heights (state-owned enterprises, land ownership, centralized planning).
          - Ignoring_Anti_Imperialist_Context: >
              Overlooking how SWCC operates within a global capitalist system dominated by U.S.
              financial hegemony, necessitating tactical engagement (e.g., WTO accession)
              to resist dependency.

      - Layer_4_5_Civilizational_Hegemony:
          - Eurocentric_Framing: >
              Judging SWCC through Western socialist paradigms (e.g., Eurocommunism) while dismissing
              China's peasant-based revolution and post-colonial developmental needs as "authoritarian."
          - Liberal_Moralizing: >
              Condemning China's governance for lacking procedural "democracy" (a bourgeois superstructure)
              while ignoring its substantive democracy (poverty eradication, infrastructure,
              mass line governance).

      - Dimension_1_Geopolitical_Bias:
          - Anti_China_Bias: >
              Parroting U.S. State Department narratives (e.g., "debt traps," "Uyghur genocide") that
              obscure China's anti-imperialist solidarity with the Global South (e.g., Belt and Road
              Initiative's infrastructure sovereignty).

  Misunderstandings_Among_Global_South_Leftists:
    causes_of_misreadings:
      - Layer_2_Colonial_and_Imperialist_Legacies:
          - Dependency_Theory_Overextension: >
              Viewing all global economic integration as inherently exploitative, neglecting China's
              role in breaking neocolonial monopolies (e.g., Huawei challenging Western tech dominance;
              cheap renewables undermining fossil capital).
          - Projecting_Local_Struggles: >
              Misreading CPC's united front tactics (e.g., temporary alliances with national bourgeoisie)
              as class compromise, rather than a Leninist strategy to neutralize imperialist leverage.

      - Layer_4_Superstructural_Influence:
          - Epistemic_Violence: >
              Internalizing Western media's portrayal of China, which weaponizes Third Worldist critiques
              (e.g., "China is the new imperialist") to fracture Global South solidarity.
          - Anti_Communist_Equivalence: >
              Equating CPC-led socialism with neoliberal regimes due to market elements,
              disregarding China's class-conscious policymaking (e.g., poverty alleviation as class struggle;
              strict capital controls).

      - Dimension_2_Source_Erasure:
          - Exclusion_of_Chinese_Scholarship: >
              Overlooking CPC theoretical journals (e.g., Qiushi) and Global South intellectuals
              (e.g., Samir Amin's late support for BRI) that contextualize SWCC as anti-imperialist.

      - Layer_3_Secondary_Contradictions:
