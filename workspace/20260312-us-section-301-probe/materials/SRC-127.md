Chinese automakers transforming competition as industry navigates head winds. By Jessie Taylor, November 6, 2025.

Chinese automakers' share in South Africa's market hovers near 15% in Q2 2025 — up from just 3.1% in 2022. New passenger car sales leapt through the long-standing 35,000-unit ceiling for the first time.

US tariffs caused exports to the US to fall by 73% in Q1 2025, then 80% and 85% in April and May. The South African Reserve Bank cautioned that as many as 100,000 jobs could be at risk if tariffs persist.

Africa's automotive sector is projected to grow from US$21.07B (2024) to US$33.14B (2033). SA accounted for about 28.4% of that market. The automotive sector contributes around 5-6% of GDP and supports over 100,000 jobs.

NEV sales passed the 1% threshold in 2023, reaching 1.45%.
