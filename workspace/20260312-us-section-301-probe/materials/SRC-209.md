Court of International Trade Orders IEEPA Tariffs to Be Refunded
Greenberg Traurig LLP, March 2026

Following the Supreme Court's ruling striking down IEEPA-based tariffs, the U.S. Court of International Trade (CIT) issued an order on March 4, 2026, directing CBP to begin refunding IEEPA tariffs to importers. The CIT's order in Atmus Filtration, Inc. v. United States marked the beginning of the practical implementation of the Supreme Court's decision.

The CIT initially ordered "immediate" refunds but subsequently stayed the order on March 6 after CBP declared its existing systems were insufficient for the scale of refunds needed (53 million entries, approximately $166 billion).

In parallel, Congress introduced the Tariff Refund Act of 2026 to establish a structured framework for processing refunds, reflecting bipartisan recognition that the administrative burden would be substantial and required legislative support.

The case highlights the gap between judicial rulings and practical implementation: even after a clear Supreme Court mandate, the mechanics of unwinding months of tariff collection present enormous logistical challenges.
