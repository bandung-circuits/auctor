## [M-B-001] Deal Value: USA Rare Earth Acquisition of Serra Verde at $2.8 Billion
**Source**: SRC-B-002
**Relevance**: Core quantitative figure for the transaction at the center of the court challenge.

> Last month, USAR announced that it would acquire Serra Verde Group, operator of the Pela Ema rare earth mine and associated processing facility in Goias state, for a total of $2.8 billion. ## [M-B-008] Brazil Rare Earth Production vs. China: 20 Tonnes vs. 270,000 Tonnes (2024)
**Source**: SRC-B-004
**Relevance**: Stark production gap between Brazil's reserves and actual output, quantifying the "high reserves, low output" paradox.

## [M-B-011] Dysprosium Price Gap: $200/kg in China vs. ~$1,000/kg Internationally
**Source**: SRC-B-004
**Relevance**: Quantitative price differential showing China's market pricing power and the strategic premium on non-Chinese supply.

> In early 2026, Chinese export controls have driven international prices for heavy rare earths like dysprosium and terbium to levels five times higher than those inside China. For example, while dysprosium sells for around $200 per kilogram in China, it costs nearly $1,000 everywhere else. This price gap exists because China still refines around 90% of the world's rare earths.
