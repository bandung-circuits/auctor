By Hanna Duggal and Marium Ali | 20 May 2025

US national debt: $36.2 trillion, 122% of GDP, growing by ~$1 trillion every three months. Trump's tax bill could add $5 trillion more. Moody's downgraded US credit.

Foreign holders: $9.05 trillion (25%). Japan $1.13T, UK $779B, China $765B. China's holdings at lowest since 2009. Both Japan and China indicated treasuries as leverage in trade negotiations.

Foreign share rose fivefold: 5% in 1970 to 25% today. Debt ceiling raised 78 times since 1960.
