By Alex Kimani, Feb 26, 2026.

The US Department of Commerce has imposed a 126% preliminary countervailing duty on Indian solar cells and modules, effectively closing off the US market. The levies follow a complaint by the Alliance for American Solar Manufacturing and Trade (AASMT), including First Solar and Hanwha Qcells. AASMT alleged Indian firms received state support through various programs including the Advance Authorisation Program, Duty Drawback Program and EPCG Scheme.

Indian solar imports to the US grew from $84 million in 2022 to nearly $793 million in 2024, thanks to PM Modi's production-linked incentive scheme (PLI). India's solar manufacturing capacity has grown 13-fold since 2020. Current capacity of ~160 GW vastly outpaces annual domestic demand at 45-50 GW. India primarily exports solar to the US, which accounted for more than 90% of total exports in 2024.

Indian firms are pivoting toward Europe, Middle East, and emerging economies. Manufacturers like Premier Energies are targeting European buyers. Waaree has 2.6 GW of manufacturing capacity in the US, plans to expand to 4.2 GW. Indian government facilitating SEZ firms to sell domestically at concessional duty rates.
