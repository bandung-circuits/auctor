## Principal Contradiction
The nations of the Global South, African and Caribbean states in particular, demand accountability for the foundational crimes that enabled capital accumulation in the core capitalist states, while those same core states refuse to acknowledge a debt whose structural consequences continue to extract wealth from Africa today through inflated borrowing costs, illicit financial flows, and commodity pricing traps.
