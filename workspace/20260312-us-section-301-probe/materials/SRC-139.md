By Ata Demirus | 11 August 2025

For Arrighi, the US acquired hegemony from the British in the fourth capital accumulation cycle. For Streeck, the current collapse continues with "a lasting interregnum — a prolonged period of social entropy."

US inequality: top 10% own vast majority of wealth (up from 61% in 1989). Bottom 50% own 2.5%. CEO-to-worker pay ratio: 278:1. Gini coefficient: 0.47 (2023).

Streeck's three "apocalyptic horsemen": Stagnation, debt, inequality. Four crisis phases since 1970s: inflation, public debt, private debt, 2008 financial crisis.

"America First" approach: tariffs, buying American provisions, reshoring incentives. But "economic effects would further weaken U.S. global power in a relative sense."

Conclusion: "a hegemonic crisis in the political economy" (Arrighi). Sharp inequality led to populism and Trump, changing course of international political economy.

References: Arrighi (2010), Streeck (2016), Milanovic (2016, 2019), Piketty (2014).
