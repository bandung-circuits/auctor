'Build first, break later': How China's green transformation was built to last
By Michael Wang, WEF, July 28, 2025

In 2024, China accounted for over 40% of global renewable energy capacity and invested $818 billion in its energy transition, more than double any other economy. China's approach is described as "xian li hou po" (build first, break later): rapidly building green energy industries while phasing down fossil fuels. Professor Elizabeth Thurbon argues: "There is no overcapacity in cheap renewables until we reach 100% renewables globally."
