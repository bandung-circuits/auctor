By Christopher Choong Weng Wai (Khazanah Research Institute, Malaysia) | Apr 11, 2025

References Stephen Miran's (CEA chair) paper "A User's Guide to Restructuring the Global Trading System": root problem is "persistent overvaluation of the dollar and asymmetric trade conditions." Core trade-off is "between export competitiveness and financial power projection."

The "Mar-a-Lago Accord" leverages tariffs to force currency accord. Operates on "the stick of tariffs" and "the carrot of the defense umbrella."

Miran's bucket criteria transcend economics: "Does the nation side with China, Russia, and Iran?" "Do the nation's leaders grandstand against the United States?"

Author's conclusion: "What is at stake is essentially US hegemony. This hegemonic order is ultimately behind the current disorder."

US hegemony constrains Global South autonomies going back to Bandung 1955.
