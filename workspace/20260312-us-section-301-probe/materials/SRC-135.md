Supreme Court Tariff Ruling: IEEPA Revenue and Potential Refunds
By Lysle Boller, Wanling Luo and Xiaoyue Sun | February 20, 2026

We project that reversing the IEEPA tariffs will generate up to $175 billion in refunds. Unless replaced by another source, future tariff revenue collections will fall by half.

In its 6-3 ruling on February 20, 2026, the Supreme Court decided that IEEPA does not grant the President the power to unilaterally impose tariffs of indefinite scope.

Cumulative IEEPA tariff revenue (Jan 2025 - Jan 2026):
- Feb 2025: $0.81B cumulative
- Apr 2025: $15.67B cumulative
- Jul 2025: $56.60B cumulative
- Oct 2025: $109.80B cumulative
- Jan 2026: $164.70B cumulative

IEEPA tariffs represent approximately half (51.9% as of January 2026) of total customs duties.

IEEPA collections by country (Nov 2025 share):
- Other: 35.45%, China: 24.16%, EU: 15.24%, Vietnam: 11.25%, India: 8.09%, Mexico: 4.05%, Canada: 1.77%

IEEPA receipts are currently about $500 million per day.

CBP reported approximately $133.5 billion in tariffs under IEEPA authority as of December 14, 2025.
