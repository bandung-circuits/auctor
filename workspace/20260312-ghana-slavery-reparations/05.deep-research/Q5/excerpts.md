# Geopolitical Dynamics — China, Asian States, and the UNGA Vote — Research Excerpts

## Question: What is China's position on the Ghana reparations resolution, and how does it relate to China's broader strategy of building influence in Africa through the Belt and Road Initiative, FOCAC, and multilateral platforms?

### [Q5-001] FOCAC 2024 Beijing Declaration explicitly notes AU reparations proclamation
**Source**: SRC-Q5-005 [new]
**Relevance**: Direct evidence of China's official acknowledgement of the AU reparations agenda at the highest diplomatic level

> China noted that the 37th AU Summit issued the Proclamation on Building a United Front to Promote the Cause of Justice and Payment of Reparations to Africans in February 2024. The Proclamation expresses opposition to historical crimes including slave trafficking, colonialism and racial segregation, and calls for bringing justice to Africa through reparations.

### [Q5-002] FOCAC 2024 Declaration frames China-Africa solidarity against colonialism and racism
**Source**: SRC-Q5-005 [new]
**Relevance**: Shows how China positions itself as an anti-colonial ally in the same declaration that references reparations

> We call on the international community to resolutely resist and combat all forms of racism and racial discrimination... China and Africa jointly call for an equal and orderly multipolar world... redressing the historical injustices done to Africa.

### [Q5-003] China-Africa shared anti-colonial identity and Global South solidarity
**Source**: SRC-Q5-008 [new]
**Relevance**: China's ambassador explicitly frames China-Africa relations through the lens of shared colonial victimhood

> "Politically, China and Africa share the same aspirations and vision. China and Africa have supported each other in fighting imperialism, colonialism and racism, based on similar suffering in recent history. In the struggle for national independence, China and Africa, as members of the Global South, have been united in their common goal, extending mutual assistance and having been the most distinctive, steadfast and major supporters of each other's anti-imperialist, anti-colonial and anti-hegemonic struggles."

### [Q5-004] Chinese Foreign Ministry spokesperson on shared anti-colonial history
**Source**: SRC-Q5-008 [new]
**Relevance**: Official Chinese government framing of China-Africa relations as rooted in shared colonial experience and demands that developed countries face historical responsibilities

> Spokesperson Lin Jian said that China and Africa endured colonialist and imperialist suppression and invasion, and the two sides supported each other and fought together on the road of anti-colonialism and anti-imperialism... China and African countries stand for bridging the gap between rich countries and poor ones, reject all forms of interference by big powers and economic coercion, and ask developed countries to face up to their historical responsibilities and deliver on their development commitments.

### [Q5-005] Ghana planning direct diplomatic engagement with China on the resolution
**Source**: SRC-G-003 [existing]
**Relevance**: Confirms Ghana is specifically targeting China as a diplomatic partner for the resolution

> Meetings have been planned with representatives from China, the Non-Aligned Movement – which includes India and Iran – and the European Union (EU).

> Talks are planned with representatives of China, and the Group of 77, a grouping of more than 130 developing countries, with around 70 additional voting members neither in the AU nor Caricom.

### [Q5-006] FOCAC 2024 support for AU membership in G20 and Africa's role in global governance
**Source**: SRC-Q5-005 [new]
**Relevance**: Shows how China's support for the reparations resolution fits into a broader strategy of backing Africa's institutional claims in multilateral forums

> China supports African countries in increasing their influence and role in global governance... China believes that Africans are qualified to serve as heads of international organizations and institutions... China applauds and welcomes more African countries to join the BRICS family.

**Assessment**: China's position is substantially documented. The 2024 FOCAC Beijing Declaration explicitly "noted" the AU reparations proclamation — a diplomatic signal of support without full endorsement. China's broader strategy is clear: framing itself as a fellow victim of colonialism and champion of Global South solidarity, while using multilateral platforms to build influence against Western hegemony. China is very likely to vote in favour of the Ghana resolution. The gap is the absence of any direct Chinese government statement specifically about the March 2026 UNGA vote.

## Question: How are major Asian states — India, Indonesia, Malaysia, Pakistan, Bangladesh — likely to vote on the resolution, given their own complex histories with colonialism and slavery, and what diplomatic outreach has Ghana conducted toward them?

### [Q5-007] Ghana's diplomatic outreach to Asian states through NAM and Commonwealth
**Source**: SRC-G-003 [existing]
**Relevance**: Direct evidence of Ghana targeting Asian states, specifically India, Pakistan, Sri Lanka through Non-Aligned Movement and Commonwealth channels

> Discussions are also planned with Non-Aligned states, which overlap with this grouping, and include Asian members such as Iran, and the Commonwealth nations of India, Pakistan and Sri Lanka.

### [Q5-008] Fiji speaking for Asia-Pacific States on slavery remembrance
**Source**: SRC-Q5-007 [new]
**Relevance**: Shows Asia-Pacific group's engagement with slavery legacy issues at UNGA level

> Fiji's delegate, speaking for the Asia-Pacific States, drew attention to modern-day slavery — over 50 million people today are estimated to live in conditions of contemporary slavery, due to armed conflicts, natural hazards, climate change, sea-level rise, pollution and environmental degradation, among other reasons.

### [Q5-009] India's prominent anti-colonial reparations discourse (Shashi Tharoor)
**Source**: SRC-E-005 [existing]
**Relevance**: India has a strong domestic discourse on reparations from British colonialism, making it sympathetic to the resolution's framing

> [Referenced in search results: Shashi Tharoor's viral 2015 Oxford Union speech arguing Britain owes reparations to India was viewed over 11 million times and became a touchstone of anti-colonial discourse. India's own colonial reparations argument — estimating $45 trillion looted from India during British rule — aligns with the logic of the Ghana resolution.]

### [Q5-010] NAM and G77+China as diplomatic channels for Asian engagement
**Source**: SRC-I-004 [existing]
**Relevance**: Shows the institutional channels through which Ghana is reaching Asian states

> Diplomatic engagements will intensify in New York beginning February 20, 2026, as Ghana consults with CARICOM, the Non-Aligned Movement, the Group of 77 and China, the European Union and other regional groupings to build broad international support.

**Assessment**: Partially answered. Ghana's diplomatic outreach to Asian states is documented through the NAM and Commonwealth channels. India, Pakistan, and Sri Lanka are specifically mentioned as targets. India's own anti-colonial reparations discourse (Tharoor) makes it likely to be sympathetic. However, there is no documented response from any Asian government to the Ghana resolution specifically. No information was found on Indonesia, Malaysia, or Bangladesh's positions. The likely voting pattern must be inferred from these states' general G77+China and NAM membership and their own colonial histories.

## Question: What positions have Latin American states (Brazil, Mexico, Colombia, Bolivia) taken, given that Brazil was the largest single destination for enslaved Africans and has its own internal reparations debate?

### [Q5-011] Colombia's VP Francia Marquez calls for global reparations fund at UN
**Source**: SRC-Q5-006 [new]
**Relevance**: Colombia's highest-ranking Black official directly advocates for reparations at the UN forum

> Colombia's Vice President Francia Marquez, an advocate of slavery reparations, urged former colonial powers to take responsibility for their past wrongs and called for a global reparations fund, at a United Nations forum on Monday.
>
> "Colonial states, states that fostered... that form of domination based on the definition of race have to assume the responsibility of restoring the human dignity of people of African descent," Marquez earlier told Reuters in an interview.

### [Q5-012] Brazil's Minister of Racial Equality at UN forum supports reparations
**Source**: SRC-Q5-006 [new]
**Relevance**: Brazil's official position at the UN forum is explicitly pro-reparations

> Brazil's Minister of Racial Equality Anielle Franco was among other politicians attending the four-day UN forum. Franco said slavery was a crime against humanity and "must be faced with courage".

### [Q5-013] Brazil formally apologizes for slavery (November 2024)
**Source**: SRC-Q5-009 [new]
**Relevance**: Brazil's federal government under Lula has taken the unprecedented step of formally apologizing for slavery

> On November 21, 2024, the Brazilian federal government (União) formally apologized for the enslavement of people in an event organized by the Advocacia-Geral da União (AGU), the Ministry of Racial Equality and the organization Educafro Brasil. "A União manifesta publicamente seu pedido de desculpas pela escravização das pessoas negras, bem como de seus efeitos."

### [Q5-014] Brazil's massive slavery legacy and reparations estimates
**Source**: SRC-Q5-009 [new]
**Relevance**: Quantifies Brazil's unique position as the largest recipient of enslaved Africans

> Brazil is, by far, the country that received the largest contingent of enslaved Africans. According to the Slave Voyages Database, 4.86 million enslaved people were disembarked in Brazilian territory between 1501 and 1900. According to a study by the University of West Indies, in partnership with the American Society of International Laws, the reparations value for slavery in Brazil could reach R$135 trillion.

### [Q5-015] Venezuela and Mexico demand reparations from Spain
**Source**: SRC-F-001 [existing]
**Relevance**: Venezuela and Mexico have both made explicit reparations demands against Spain

> In 2019, then-Mexican President Andres Manuel Lopez Obrador sent a letter to Spain's King Felipe and Pope Francis requesting they apologise to Mexico's indigenous peoples for abuses committed during the Spanish conquest. Madrid did not reply but said it "firmly rejected" its content. On Oct. 14, Venezuela's Nicolas Maduro said that his country, along with other Latin American nations, intended to "make a profound claim" for Spain to pay reparations for "the theft and plundering of our lands as well as the slavery and suffering endured by our indigenous and African grandparents".

### [Q5-016] Suriname speaks for Latin American and Caribbean States on slavery
**Source**: SRC-Q5-007 [new]
**Relevance**: Shows GRULAC solidarity on the slavery remembrance issue

> Suriname's delegate, speaking on behalf of the Latin American and Caribbean States, paid tribute to the Indigenous people who suffered and perished due to the transatlantic slave trade.

### [Q5-017] Brazil's reparations obligations estimated at $4 trillion
**Source**: SRC-B-002 [existing]
**Relevance**: Brazil itself has estimated reparations obligations for its own practice of slavery

> Robinson said that... Portugal [is required to pay] US$20 trillion to Brazil, which is also required to pay about four trillion dollars in respect to its practice of TCS in Brazil from 1822 to 1888.

**Assessment**: Well answered. Latin American states, particularly Brazil and Colombia, have taken strong pro-reparations positions. Brazil formally apologized for slavery in November 2024 and sent its Minister of Racial Equality to the UN forum. Colombia's VP Marquez called for a global reparations fund. Venezuela and Mexico have demanded reparations from Spain. Brazil's unique dual position — as both a destination for enslaved Africans and a practitioner of slavery — adds complexity but does not diminish its support. These states are very likely to vote in favour. Bolivia's specific position was not found.

## Question: How does the US position on the resolution connect to domestic US politics, particularly the African-American reparations debate (HR 40), and how might the Trump administration's stance differ from previous US positions?

### [Q5-018] Trump administration withdraws US from UN Permanent Forum on People of African Descent
**Source**: SRC-Q5-004 [new]
**Relevance**: Direct evidence of Trump's hostile stance toward the UN reparations machinery

> Donald Trump has signed an executive order to pull the US out of a "racist" UN forum accused of pushing a "global reparations agenda".
>
> State Department spokesman Tommy Pigott declared: "America will no longer lend its credibility to racist organisations".
>
> He added: "Radical activists who embrace DEI ideology and seek to compel the United States to adopt policies mandating race-based wealth redistribution, in organisations such as the UN Permanent Forum on People of African Descent will no longer be entertained."

### [Q5-019] Trump's explicit rejection of reparations
**Source**: SRC-Q5-003 [new]
**Relevance**: Trump's personal stance on reparations

> When asked about federal reparations in a 2019 interview with The Hill, Trump said, "I don't see it happening."
>
> The White House has not responded to a request for comment about the bill.

### [Q5-020] HR 40 reintroduced despite hostile political environment
**Source**: SRC-Q5-003 [new]
**Relevance**: Shows the domestic reparations debate continuing under Trump despite opposition

> Rep. Ayanna Pressley will reintroduce H.R. 40, federal legislation to study reparations for slavery, on Wednesday as the Trump administration leads a wide-scale rollback of diversity, equity and inclusion efforts in the federal government.
>
> The bill, which had 130 co-sponsors in the last session, is not likely to advance under the Republican-controlled Congress, and the White House has previously been opposed to any reparations efforts.

### [Q5-021] Previous US position under Biden more supportive
**Source**: SRC-F-001 [existing]
**Relevance**: Contrast between Biden and Trump administrations on reparations

> At the federal level, a proposal to create a commission to study what reparations might look like has been stalled in Congress for 35 years... A U.S. State Department spokesperson said the country "strongly supports" the U.N. Permanent Forum on People of African Descent, which has called for the creation of an international tribunal on reparations. President Joe Biden said in 2021 he supported a study on whether descendants of enslaved people in the United States should receive reparations.

### [Q5-022] Trump administration's pattern of opposing multilateral initiatives at UNGA
**Source**: SRC-Q5-007 [new]
**Relevance**: Demonstrates the Trump administration's broader pattern of voting against or isolating itself at UNGA

> [On the Nutrition Decade extension] adopted by a recorded vote of 158 in favour to 1 against (United States), with 0 abstentions. The representative of the United States, who called for a vote on the text, said it contains terms and concepts that promote "advance harmful gender ideology and defiance of biological reality".
>
> [On conflict diamonds] An overwhelming majority — 144 Member States — voted in favour of retaining those [SDG] paragraphs, defeating two negative votes cast by the United States and Argentina.

### [Q5-023] Deep racial divide on reparations in US public opinion
**Source**: SRC-Q5-003 [new]
**Relevance**: Shows the domestic political dynamics constraining any US administration on reparations

> In 2021, 77% of Black Americans said they supported issuing reparations tied to slavery, while only 18% of white Americans agreed, according to the Pew Research Center.

### [Q5-024] US reparations debate has deep historical roots
**Source**: SRC-E-005 [existing]
**Relevance**: Academic analysis of US reparations politics

> Despite my recommendations above, I think it very unlikely that the US government would offer reparations of any kind to Africa... H.R. 40 — Commission to Study and Develop Reparation Proposals for African-Americans Act.

**Assessment**: Fully answered. The Trump administration's position is clear: it has withdrawn from the UN Permanent Forum on People of African Descent (January 2026), framing it as a "racist" organization pushing "DEI ideology." This represents a dramatic shift from the Biden administration, which "strongly supported" the same forum. Trump has personally dismissed reparations. The US is almost certain to vote against or abstain on the Ghana resolution. HR 40 continues to be reintroduced but faces a hostile Congress. The domestic politics — with 77% Black support but only 18% white support for reparations — explain the structural constraints.

## Question: What are the specific voting dynamics within the G77+China bloc on this resolution — are there internal divisions, and which states might abstain or vote against despite bloc solidarity expectations?

### [Q5-025] Vote arithmetic: AU + CARICOM insufficient for majority
**Source**: SRC-G-003 [existing]
**Relevance**: Establishes the basic vote count and the necessity of G77+China support

> It is believed that Ghana's resolution can count on support from the 15 member states of Caricom, and the 55 AU members, although some, like Sudan, are currently suspended from the bloc amid ongoing conflict.
>
> This would fall short of the 97 ordinarily required for a simple majority in the UN General Assembly.
>
> Talks are planned with representatives of China, and the Group of 77, a grouping of more than 130 developing countries, with around 70 additional voting members neither in the AU nor Caricom.

### [Q5-026] Ghana's diplomatic strategy targeting multiple blocs
**Source**: SRC-I-009 [existing]
**Relevance**: Shows the full range of Ghana's diplomatic outreach

> Beginning 20 February 2026, Ghana will conduct intensive diplomatic engagements in New York with CARICOM, the Non-Aligned Movement, the Group of 77 in China, the European Union, and other regional groupings. Informal consultations on the draft text are expected to take place between 23 February and 12 March 2026. Our objective is simple, to build a broad consensus behind this resolution.

### [Q5-027] UNGA voting patterns showing US isolation on development issues
**Source**: SRC-Q5-007 [new]
**Relevance**: Shows that the US is typically isolated when voting against Global South priorities, suggesting strong G77+China cohesion

> [Nutrition resolution] adopted by a recorded vote of 158 in favour to 1 against (United States), with 0 abstentions.
>
> [Conflict diamonds SDG language] An overwhelming majority — 144 Member States — voted in favour... defeating two negative votes cast by the United States and Argentina.

### [Q5-028] Concerns about intra-African unity
**Source**: Initial Summary [existing context]
**Relevance**: Notes internal AU divisions that could extend to broader G77+China dynamics

> While 40 AU member states formally support the resolution, the September 2025 reparations summit in Addis Ababa saw low turnout of African heads of state. Some member states privately prefer bilateral diplomacy over collective action.

### [Q5-029] FOCAC Declaration shows G77+China and NAM solidarity framework
**Source**: SRC-Q5-005 [new]
**Relevance**: The institutional framework that would underpin G77+China bloc cohesion on this vote

> China commends Uganda for successfully convening the 19th Non-Aligned Movement Summit and the G77 plus China Third South Summit in January 2024 to advance solidarity and cooperation of the Global South. China and Africa, as the biggest developing country and the continent home to the largest number of developing countries, belong to the Global South. We invariably stand in solidarity with fellow developing countries through thick and thin.

**Assessment**: Partially answered. The vote arithmetic is clear (97 needed, AU+CARICOM have ~70). The G77+China bloc's general solidarity on anti-colonial and development issues is well-documented. UNGA voting records show the US is typically isolated on such votes. However, specific internal divisions within G77+China on this particular resolution are not documented. Potential abstainers could include: (a) Arab states with complex positions on the trans-Saharan slave trade; (b) states with strong economic ties to Western powers who might face diplomatic pressure; (c) Asian states with no direct connection to the transatlantic trade who might see the issue as outside their concerns. No specific state-level intelligence on intended abstentions was found.

## Question: How does Russia position itself on the resolution, given its strategic interests in Africa and its own anti-Western diplomatic posture?

### [Q5-030] Russia's official statement explicitly supporting AU reparations agenda
**Source**: SRC-Q5-002 [new]
**Relevance**: Direct Russian government statement at a UN side event supporting reparations

> We support the decision of African Union to designate 2025 as the year of "Justice for Africans and People of African Descent through Reparations". This focus opens up opportunities for cooperation with the continent on the preservation of historical memory, with a focus on combating neocolonialism.

### [Q5-031] Russia frames reparations as anti-Western tool
**Source**: SRC-Q5-002 [new]
**Relevance**: Russia explicitly positions the reparations issue as a critique of Western colonial crimes

> The Africa bore the full brunt of colonial crimes perpetrated by the Western countries... This declaration was promoted by the USSR, and thus the United Nations, with the help of my country, contributed greatly to the decolonization process in Africa.
>
> Today, we all need to develop concrete measures aimed at compensating damages and providing reparations to countries that suffered at the hands of Western colonial policies.

### [Q5-032] Russia takes practical steps on related UN initiatives
**Source**: SRC-Q5-002 [new]
**Relevance**: Shows Russia is not merely rhetorical but actively pushes related agenda items at the UN

> In particular, the Russian Federation as a member of the Group of Friends in Defense of the Charter of the United Nations has put forward resolution "International Day against Colonialism in All Its Forms and Manifestations".
>
> In the United Nations General Assembly resolution "Return or restitution of cultural property to the countries of origin", Russia actively promoted a paragraph on illicit import, export and transfer of ownership of cultural property.

### [Q5-033] Russia supports recognizing colonialism as a crime against humanity
**Source**: SRC-Q5-002 [new]
**Relevance**: Russia goes beyond the Ghana resolution's scope by advocating for colonialism as a crime against humanity in a UN convention

> Another step towards justice for people of African descent would be to recognize the crime of colonialism as a crime against humanity. The General Assembly will start working on the elaboration of the convention on crimes against humanity in accordance with General Assembly resolution A/RES/79/122. The African Union agreed that colonial crimes must be clarified as crimes against humanity. This understanding should be reflected in the above-mentioned UN convention.

### [Q5-034] Russia frames support within multipolar world order framework
**Source**: SRC-Q5-002 [new]
**Relevance**: Russia links reparations support to its broader foreign policy concept

> Our country's commitment to facilitating Africa's emergence as a distinctive and influential center of global development is also enshrined as a priority in the Concept of the Foreign Policy of the Russian Federation. We consistently support strengthening the continent's position in the emerging multipolar world order.

### [Q5-035] Russia's African partners actively support reparations
**Source**: SRC-Q5-001 [new]
**Relevance**: Russia's key African partner (CAR) is explicitly aligned on reparations

> The Central African Republic (CAR), a member of the AU and a key partner of Russia in the region, supports this collective stance. The CAR actively advocates for the "decolonization" of its economy and historical accountability, aligning with its current political strategy to reduce the influence of former colonial powers, particularly France, and to bolster national sovereignty.

### [Q5-036] Russian state media amplifies the reparations narrative
**Source**: SRC-Q5-001 [new]
**Relevance**: TASS coverage frames reparations as a legitimate demand, using a State Duma expert as source

> According to various estimates, potential reparations from European nations to African countries for the legacy of colonialism could range from $18 trillion to an astonishing $100 trillion. Galli Monastyreva, Director of Humanitarian Projects at the Association for Economic Cooperation with African Countries (AECAC) and a member of the State Duma's Expert Council on Africa, shared these figures with TASS.

**Assessment**: Fully answered. Russia's position is unambiguously supportive. The September 2025 statement by Deputy Permanent Representative Zabolotskaya at the UN side event co-organized by the AU explicitly supports the reparations agenda, frames it as an anti-Western colonial justice issue, and links it to Russia's broader strategy of building a multipolar world order. Russia has taken concrete steps (proposing anti-colonialism resolutions, promoting cultural property restitution). Russian state media amplifies the narrative. Russia is certain to vote in favour.
