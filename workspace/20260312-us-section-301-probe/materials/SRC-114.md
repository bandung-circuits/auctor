India's Solar Manufacturers Slapped With 126% U.S. Tariff
By Alex Kimani, OilPrice.com, February 26, 2026

The U.S. Department of Commerce imposed a 126% preliminary countervailing duty on Indian solar cells and modules, closing off the U.S. market. Indian solar imports to the U.S. grew from $84 million in 2022 to nearly $793 million in 2024. India's solar module manufacturing capacity of ~160 GW vastly outpaces annual domestic demand of 45-50 GW. The U.S. accounted for more than 90% of India's total solar exports in 2024.
