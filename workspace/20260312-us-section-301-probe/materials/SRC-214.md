Countdown to US tariff, 7 Thai industries at risk, millions of jobs on the line

July 23, 2025

Thailand is on edge ahead of the August 1, 2025 deadline, when the US is set to decide on whether to maintain a 36% tariff on Thai exports.

Labour-intensive industries such as textiles, garments, gems and jewellery, electronics, electrical appliances, processed foods, and rubber products could suffer a significant blow.

More than 400,000 garment workers in the crosshairs. The jewellery sector: 700,000 to 800,000 workers. Electronics sector: 600,000 workers. Rubber products: over 100,000 workers.

"Vietnam essentially gave everything up by slashing its tariffs on US imports to zero. Thailand can't afford to match that without severe repercussions, particularly for our agriculture sector."

According to initial estimates, 1-2 million workers are directly engaged in export-related industries, with several million more in the agricultural sector and 2-3 million in related supply chains.

"If these workers lose their jobs, it will be very difficult to attract the next generation into the industry."
