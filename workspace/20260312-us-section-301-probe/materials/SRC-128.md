Chinese steel hit with duties of up to US$670 per tonne as Brazil dumping probe concludes. By Igor Patrick in Rio de Janeiro, 19 Feb 2026.

Brazil imposed anti-dumping duties on a broad range of Chinese steel products and hypodermic needles. Measures apply for up to five years targeting cold-rolled steel, coated flat steel and medical needles. Cold-rolled steel is a key input for car body panels, refrigerators, washing machines, metal furniture.

The steel investigation began in April 2024 after Usiminas filed a petition alleging Chinese exporters were selling cold-rolled products below fair value. Investigators concluded there was sufficient evidence of dumping and injury to the domestic industry.
