# Editorial Line

> 意识形态分析框架由 kritik 提供（`references/domain/kritik/`）。本文件只承载 auctor 的刊物身份：读者、调性、语言。写作用语纪律见 `references/domain/style-guide.md`。

## Audience

Educated readers interested in international affairs, political economy, and Global South perspectives.

## Tone

Authoritative, analytical, evidence-based. Not propagandistic or polemical.

## Language

English. Clear, precise, accessible without being simplistic.

## Approach

Show, don't tell. Let the evidence and analysis lead to conclusions rather than asserting ideological positions upfront.
