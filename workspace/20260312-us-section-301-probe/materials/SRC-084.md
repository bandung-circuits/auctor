Wanted in Budget 2026: A Trump shock absorber for the small enterprises to survive

The article discusses the need for India's Union Budget 2026 to include measures to protect micro, small and medium enterprises (MSMEs) from the impact of US tariffs and global trade tensions. India's MSMEs, which contribute approximately 30% to GDP and 45% to exports, face significant challenges from Trump-era tariffs and trade volatility. The article calls for a dedicated Trade Resilience Fund, enhanced export credit facilities, market diversification support, and technology upgradation schemes to help small enterprises survive the ongoing trade disruption.
