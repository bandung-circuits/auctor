Countdown to US tariff, 7 Thai industries at risk, millions of jobs on the line
The Nation Thailand, July 23, 2025

Thai private sector braces for US tariff verdict as labour-intensive industries fear competitiveness blow. Thailand faces a 36% US tariff, putting it at a disadvantage against regional rivals like Vietnam (20%) and Indonesia (19%).

Seven key labor-intensive industries are particularly vulnerable: textiles (400,000 workers), garments, gems and jewellery (700,000-800,000 workers), electronics (600,000 workers), electrical appliances, processed foods, and rubber products (100,000+ workers). According to estimates, 1-2 million workers are directly engaged in export-related industries, with several million more in agriculture and 2-3 million in related supply chains.

Industry leaders warn that if the 36% tariff is upheld, it could trigger a shift of manufacturing orders and foreign investment to competing nations. Thailand cannot match Vietnam's approach of slashing tariffs to zero on US imports and committing to buying tens of billions of dollars' worth of American goods, particularly for the agriculture sector which supports a large segment of the population.

The US remains concerned about Chinese goods being rerouted through Thai exporters, and geopolitical considerations like the US seeking to establish a military presence in Thailand.
