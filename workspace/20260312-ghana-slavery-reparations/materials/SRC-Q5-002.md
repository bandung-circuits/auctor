Statement by the Deputy Permanent Representative Maria Zabolotskaya at the High-Level side event "The United Nations 80 Years Later: Accelerating the Reform Agenda and Strengthening the Momentum towards Justice and Reparations"

23 September 2025

Excellencies,

I would like to start by thanking the African Union and the Togolese Republic for organizing today's important event. It is a valuable opportunity to confirm our commitment to truth and justice. Especially in these days when we increasingly encounter attempts to falsify and rewrite shameful and inconvenient pages of history to suit opportunistic geopolitical considerations.

The Africa bore the full brunt of colonial crimes perpetrated by the Western countries. The burden that the era of colonial conquest imposed on the region's development is difficult to appreciate even today, more than half a century after the adoption of the UN Declaration on the Granting of Independence to Colonial Countries and Peoples in 1960. This declaration was promoted by the USSR, and thus the United Nations, with the help of my country, contributed greatly to the decolonization process in Africa.

We support the decision of African Union to designate 2025 as the year of "Justice for Africans and People of African Descent through Reparations". This focus opens up opportunities for cooperation with the continent on the preservation of historical memory, with a focus on combating neocolonialism. We take this issue seriously and take practical steps to promote this agenda. In particular, the Russian Federation as a member of the Group of Friends in Defense of the Charter of the United Nations has put forward resolution "International Day against Colonialism in All Its Forms and Manifestations".

Today, we all need to develop concrete measures aimed at compensating damages and providing reparations to countries that suffered at the hands of Western colonial policies. In the United Nations General Assembly resolution "Return or restitution of cultural property to the countries of origin", Russia actively promoted a paragraph on illicit import, export and transfer of ownership of cultural property, which are part of museums and collections, and calls upon Member States to take the appropriate steps for their return or restitution to the countries of origin. This position should be used by African countries that suffered from colonialism.

Another step towards justice for people of African descent would be to recognize the crime of colonialism as a crime against humanity. The General Assembly will start working on the elaboration of the convention on crimes against humanity in accordance with General Assembly resolution A/RES/79/122. The African Union agreed that colonial crimes must be clarified as crimes against humanity. This understanding should be reflected in the above-mentioned UN convention.

Our country's commitment to facilitating Africa's emergence as a distinctive and influential center of global development is also enshrined as a priority in the Concept of the Foreign Policy of the Russian Federation. We consistently support strengthening the continent's position in the emerging multipolar world order.

We are convinced that true solidarity in international relation must consist of joint efforts to prevent the resurgence of negative phenomena, rather than attempts to silence or ignore them.

I thank you.
