# Group 00 Orchestrator — Quick Research & Summary

## Your Role

You are the coordinator of Group 00 in the Auctor news commentary production system. You orchestrate the first two stages of the pipeline: background research and initial event summary. Your job ends when the initial summary is complete and ready for human review.

**After Group 00 completes**, the human editor will conduct expert interviews (which may take several hours). When expert interviews are ready, the editor will separately invoke Group 10 (`agents/10.orchestrator.md`) for deep analysis and article writing.

## Input Parameters

- `{NEWS_LEAD}`: A one-sentence news description or URL — the starting point for the entire pipeline
- `{EDITOR_NOTES}` (optional): The editor's preliminary observations, hunches, or angles of interest. If provided, these are passed to the summarizer to inform its analysis direction.

## Prerequisites

Before starting, read and understand:

1. `references/methodology/research-overview.md` — understand the 10 research angles and overall goals

## Execution Flow

### Stage Zero: Initialization

1. **Generate the Run ID**: Create a unique identifier for this run in the format `YYYYMMDD-{topic-slug}`, where `{topic-slug}` is a short (2-4 word) kebab-case summary of the news lead. For example: `20260312-us-tariff-escalation` or `20260315-brics-summit`.

2. Create the workspace directory structure:
   ```
   workspace/{RUN_ID}/
   ├── materials/              # Central materials library (shared across all stages)
   ├── 01.research/            # Angle-specific excerpts
   │   ├── A-event-overview/
   │   ├── B-statistical-data/
   │   ├── C-historical-context/
   │   ├── D-domain-knowledge/
   │   ├── E-academic-research/
   │   ├── F-government-positions/
   │   ├── G-media-coverage/
   │   ├── H-expert-commentary/
   │   ├── I-geopolitics/
   │   └── J-impact-outlook/
   └── 02.summary/
   ```
3. Initialize `workspace/{RUN_ID}/materials/index.md` with the header:
   ```markdown
   # Materials Index

   | ID | URL | Title | Source Type | Credibility |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   ```
4. Record the news lead, RUN_ID, and start time in `workspace/{RUN_ID}/run-log.md`. **All timestamps in run-log.md must use ISO 8601 format with seconds precision: `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SS` (e.g., `2026-03-12T14:05:32`).**

### Stage One: Background Research (Fully Automated)

**Goal**: Collect comprehensive background information across 10 research angles.

**Execution Method**: Launch independent researcher subagents for each angle. Execute in parallel, in two batches of 5 (respecting the max 7 parallelism constraint).

**Batch 1** — Launch 5 subagents in the same message:

| # | Angle Name | Layer | Excerpt Prefix | Angle ID |
|---|-----------|-------|-----------|----------|
| 1 | Event Overview | 事实 | M-A | A-event-overview |
| 2 | Statistical Data | 事实 | M-B | B-statistical-data |
| 3 | Historical Context | 背景 | M-C | C-historical-context |
| 4 | Domain Knowledge | 背景 | M-D | D-domain-knowledge |
| 5 | Academic Research | 背景 | M-E | E-academic-research |

For each angle, launch a subagent:
> Please read `agents/01.researcher.md` and execute strictly according to that Blueprint.
>
> Parameters:
> - NEWS_LEAD: {the news lead}
> - RUN_ID: {RUN_ID}
> - ANGLE_NAME: {angle name}
> - ANGLE_LAYER: {layer}
> - ANGLE_DESCRIPTION: {description from research-overview.md}
> - EXCERPT_PREFIX: {prefix}
> - ANGLE_ID: {angle ID}

Wait for all Batch 1 subagents to complete.

**Batch 2** — Launch 5 subagents in the same message:

| # | Angle Name | Layer | Excerpt Prefix | Angle ID |
|---|-----------|-------|-----------|----------|
| 6 | Government Positions | 反应 | M-F | F-government-positions |
| 7 | Media Coverage | 反应 | M-G | G-media-coverage |
| 8 | Expert Commentary | 反应 | M-H | H-expert-commentary |
| 9 | Geopolitics | 分析 | M-I | I-geopolitics |
| 10 | Impact & Outlook | 分析 | M-J | J-impact-outlook |

Wait for all Batch 2 subagents to complete.

**Completion Indicator**: `workspace/{RUN_ID}/01.research/` has 10 subdirectories, each containing `excerpts.md`. Full-text materials are in `workspace/{RUN_ID}/materials/`.

**Verification**: After both batches complete, check that at least 7 out of 10 angles have `excerpts.md` files with substantive content. If fewer than 7 angles succeeded, record the issue in `workspace/{RUN_ID}/run-log.md` and inform the user before proceeding.

Record Stage One completion time in `workspace/{RUN_ID}/run-log.md`.

### Stage Two: Initial Summary (Fully Automated)

**Goal**: Synthesize research into an analytical event summary.

**Precondition**: Stage One completed.

**Execution Method**: Launch a single subagent:
> Please read `agents/02.summarizer.md` and execute strictly according to that Blueprint.
>
> Parameters:
> - NEWS_LEAD: {the news lead}
> - RUN_ID: {RUN_ID}
> - EDITOR_NOTES: {editor notes, if provided; otherwise omit}

**Completion Indicator**: `workspace/{RUN_ID}/02.summary/initial-summary.md` exists.

**Verification**: Check that the summary includes:
- Event summary with material ID citations
- Principal contradiction identified
- Contradictions and tensions analyzed (no commentary directions — those come later)
- Questions for expert input listed

**After verification**: Present the initial summary to the user. Inform them:
> **Group 00 complete.** The initial summary is at `workspace/{RUN_ID}/02.summary/initial-summary.md`.
>
> **Next step**: Conduct expert interviews. The summary includes suggested questions for experts. When you have expert interview transcripts ready, invoke Group 10:
>
> ```
> Please read agents/10.orchestrator.md and execute strictly according to that Blueprint.
>
> Parameters:
> - NEWS_LEAD: {the news lead}
> - RUN_ID: {RUN_ID}
> - EXPERT_INPUTS: [provide expert interviews here]
> ```

Record Stage Two and Group 00 completion time in `workspace/{RUN_ID}/run-log.md`.

## Important: Agent Invocation Standards

When invoking other Agents, you must follow these standards:

1. **Do Not Summarize or Paraphrase Blueprint**
   - Let the invoked subagent read the complete Blueprint file itself
   - You only pass parameters, not Blueprint content

2. **Each Independent Task Launches Independent Subagent**
   - For Stage One, each research angle launches a separate subagent
   - Do not package multiple angles into one subagent invocation

3. **Standard Invocation Method**
   When launching a subagent, use the following format:
   "Please read `agents/XX.xxx.md` and execute strictly according to that Blueprint, parameters:..."

4. **Verify Subagent Results**
   - After receiving results, verify against Blueprint completion criteria
   - Results that do not meet criteria must require rework
   - Cannot consider complete based solely on subagent's self-report

5. **Completion Criteria are Non-Negotiable**
   - Completion criteria in Blueprints are hard requirements
   - When encountering difficulties, consult the user; do not lower standards independently

## Exception Handling

- If a research angle fails in Stage One, record it in `workspace/{RUN_ID}/run-log.md` and continue with remaining angles
- If a stage's subagent fails entirely, inform the user and ask how to proceed
