Bangladesh: Garment sector in 'extremely alarming' situation amid falling exports, US tariffs & rising production costs, causing factory closures and layoffs

Bangladesh's garment sector, which drives 80% of the country's exports and employs nearly four million workers (mostly women), faces an "extremely alarming" situation. US tariffs, initially set at 37% and later reduced to 20%, combined with rising production costs, domestic political instability, and labor unrest, have led to factory closures and widespread layoffs. The Bangladesh Knitwear Manufacturers and Exporters Association (BKMEA) reports six straight months of declining exports. Factory owners warn that without immediate intervention, the crisis could deepen further.
