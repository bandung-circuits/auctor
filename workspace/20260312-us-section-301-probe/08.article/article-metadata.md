## Principal Contradiction

The United States' shrinking share of global productive capacity is colliding with its determination to maintain coercive dominance over the international trade architecture, producing a structural pattern of crisis externalization in which the costs fall overwhelmingly on workers, farmers, and small producers across the Global South.
