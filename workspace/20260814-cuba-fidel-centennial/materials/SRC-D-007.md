International Colloquium "Fidel: Legacy and Future" Set for August Inauguration in Havana

The objective is to promote a critical and pluralistic debate on the contributions of the historic leader to contemporary thought on the centenary of his birth. Photo: JR.

February 4, 2026 Hour: 3:03 am

An international colloquium marking Fidel Castro's centennial will gather scholars and cultural figures in Cuba to debate his revolutionary legacy and global impact.

The First Edition of the International Colloquium "Fidel: Legacy and Future" will be held from August 10 to 13, 2026, at Havana's Convention Palace, as part of the commemorations for the centennial of the birth of Commander in Chief of the Cuban Revolution Fidel Castro Ruz.

The call, initially announced last January 27 by Litza Elena Gonzalez Desdin, national president of the University Students Federation (FEU, in Spanish), aims to bring together scholars, academics, and personalities from various parts of the world.

The announcement has already sparked early international interest. Within the first 24 hours of its publication on the digital platforms of the Fidel Castro Ruz Center, more than 50 emails were received from potential participants. Inquiries have come primarily from the United States, Spain, and Mexico followed by nations such as Peru and Ecuador.

"This event will take place precisely during the days when our country celebrates Fidel's centennial. And this is not by chance," stated deputy director of the Fidel Castro Ruz Center, Sissi Abay Diaz Abay Díaz.

The stated goal is to create a space for "plural debate, within a framework of respect", that will serve as the culminating point of the anniversary activities, attracting participants from around the world to to reflect on the revolutionary ideology of the Cuban historic leader.

With an inclusive philosophy, the call has been designed in an "open-ended" manner and with "fairly broad thematic axes." This, according to the organizer, aims to encourage the presentation of papers that are "as substantive as possible."

The topics to be addressed will range from "Fidel as a revolutionary and statesman" and "Fidel and the world, his humanist and internationalist legacy", to "his cultural imprint", "the military strategist", "Fidel and imperialism", "unity as a strategy" and "education, science, and consciousness."

The colloquium will not be limited to theoretical sessions. It will include panels, workshops, and specific forums for youth, parliamentarians, and women.

Books on Fidel Castro published in Cuba and abroad will be presented. As a special contribution from the Center that bears his name, the edition of the "Selected Works of the Commander in Chief" will be presented -a four-year editorial project that, according to Abay Diaz, "summarizes what is essential to understanding Fidel's thought."

The Institute of Cuban History, the Juan Marinello Institute, and the Office of Historical Affairs have joined as co-sponsors. This collaboration will turn the event into a broad platform integrating exhibitions, film screenings, documentaries, and other parallel events, such as the traditional Colloquium on the Cuban Revolution organized by the Institute of History.

Abay Diaz acknowledged that organizing an event of this magnitude amid the complex economic situation on the island "constitutes a challenge," given the logistical and international mobility demands it entails. However, she emphasized that this challenge "is secondary or insignificant compared to the need we have today to engage with Fidel's ideology."

"Many of the teachings he left us, contained in his work and his prose, can allow us to confront precisely the complex situations we are experiencing," she stated.

The event program will include a meeting of the Fidel Castro Ruz Chairs, a symposium on the Cuban Revolution, and other sectoral forums (youth forum, parliamentary forum, women's forum, forum of the network of artists, intellectuals, and social movements in defense of humanity and the anti-fascist international, among others).

Keynote speeches, thematic conferences, round tables, panels, presentations, book presentations, documentaries, or related cultural projects are the forms of participation, through abstracts in Spanish or English that must be send before April 30.

The debates and conclusions will be compiled in a publication so that the ideas generated at this event can fuel the struggle for the "better future" to which Fidel dedicated his life.
