# Output Template

## Article Structure

The final article should follow this general structure (sections may be adapted to the specific topic):

```
# [Article Title]

## [Opening Section]
- Hook: Start with a concrete, vivid detail or striking fact
- Context: Situate the event in its immediate circumstances
- Thesis: Signal the analytical direction without stating it as ideology

## [Factual Foundation]
- Detailed account of the event
- Key data and statistics
- Timeline of developments

## [Historical/Structural Context]
- Historical background that illuminates current dynamics
- Structural factors: economic, political, institutional
- Relevant policy frameworks and their evolution

## [Analysis Section(s)]
- Principal contradiction and its manifestations
- Power dynamics and class/material interests
- Comparison of different actors' positions and motivations
- Challenge to dominant narratives with evidence

## [Global South Perspective]
- Impact on and implications for the Global South
- Agency and responses from Global South actors
- Connections to broader patterns of imperialism/resistance

## [Conclusion]
- Forward-looking analysis: what this means going forward
- Implications for the target audience
- Do NOT merely summarize; offer analytical insight
```

## Format Specifications

### Headings
- Level 1 heading (#): Article title only, one per document
- Level 2 heading (##): Major sections
- Level 3 heading (###): Subsections

### Citations
- Reference materials using material IDs: [M-A-003]
- Direct quotes use > blockquote format with attribution
- All data claims must cite their source

### Length
- Target: approximately 3,000 words
- Each major section: 400-700 words
- Flexible based on topic complexity

### Language
- English
- Follow the style requirements in `references/domain/style-guide.md`

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] Article has a clear thesis that emerges from evidence
- [ ] Every major claim is supported by cited evidence
- [ ] Historical/structural context is provided, not just surface-level reporting
- [ ] Global South perspectives are centered, not peripheral
- [ ] Opposing arguments are presented fairly before being critiqued
- [ ] Conclusion offers forward-looking analysis, not just summary
- [ ] Material IDs are used consistently for traceability
- [ ] Language is analytical, not propagandistic or polemical
- [ ] Length is approximately 3,000 words
