Cambodia: Garment workers predict bleak future with US tariffs

Cambodia faces a 49% US tariff rate, one of the highest imposed under Trump's "Liberation Day" tariffs. The country's garment sector, which employs hundreds of thousands of workers, is particularly vulnerable. Workers and factory owners predict a bleak future as orders decline and production shifts to countries with lower tariff rates. The garment industry is a critical source of employment and export revenue for Cambodia, and the tariffs threaten to undermine decades of economic progress.
