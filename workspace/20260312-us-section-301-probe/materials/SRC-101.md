# [SRC-101] Trump's Tariffs Are Expected to Undermine the Clean Energy Transition

**Source URL**: https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/trumps-tariffs-are-expected-to-undermine-the-clean-energy-transition/
**Fetch Time**: 2026-03-12T22:45:00Z

**Credibility**: Medium-High

**Original Content**:

By Benjamin Storrow, Sara Schonhardt, Mike Lee & E&E News. April 2, 2025.

Noah Kaufman, Columbia climate economist: "It just seems like where we are headed is totally uncharted." Antoine Vagneur-Jones, BloombergNEF: "This isn't a thing which is just good for renewables, bad for fossil fuels, or good for fossil fuels, bad for renewables. It's a period of massive uncertainty."

Shortages of electrical components have persisted for 54 consecutive months. US imported $31.3B wire/cable (52% Mexico), $29.2B power supplies/transformers (21% Mexico, 13% China), $13.3B electric motors/generators (32% Mexico, 13% China) in 2024.

China may find new markets: "you could conceivably see a world where the energy transition starts to accelerate slightly in some of those poorer countries where it was seemingly more of a rich country thing" (Vagneur-Jones). Tariffs could add 15% to average vehicle costs. "You're talking years of pain" (Sam Abuelsamid, Telemetry Insight).

US solar module production grew from 14.5 GW (2023) to 50 GW (early 2025) under IRA, but still mostly uses imported components. Michael Carr, Solar Energy Manufacturers for America: "Tariffs can be a part of the solution set, but they're not necessarily dependable enough that you can invest against them."

Vanessa Sciarra, American Clean Power: "The policy whiplash from these tariffs will ultimately undermine the ability to realize a domestic supply chain and will constrain efforts to deliver energy security and reliability for Americans."
