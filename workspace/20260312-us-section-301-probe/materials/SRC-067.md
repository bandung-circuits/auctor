# [SRC-067] China Foreign Ministry and Commerce Ministry Response to US Section 301 Trade Probe (March 12, 2026)

**Source URL**: https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2026-03-12/doc-inhqtqkk1709068.shtml
**Fetch Time**: 2026-03-12T20:40:00Z
**Source Type**: Quality News Outlets (Sina Finance / Yicai)
**Credibility**: Medium-High

**Original Content**:

外交部发言人郭嘉昆主持3月12日例行记者会。

郭嘉昆表示，中方在处理中美经贸问题上的立场是一贯、明确的，反对各种形式的单边关税措施，关税战、贸易战不符合任何一方的利益，双方应该在平等、尊重、互惠的基础上协商解决有关问题。

郭嘉昆表示，所谓"产能过剩"是一个伪命题，中方反对以此为借口进行政治操纵。

[Translation: Foreign Ministry spokesperson Guo Jiakun at the March 12 press conference stated: China's position on China-US economic and trade issues is consistent and clear — opposing all forms of unilateral tariff measures. A tariff war or trade war serves neither side's interests. The so-called "overcapacity" is a false proposition, and China opposes using it as a pretext for political manipulation.]

商务部新闻发言人表示，中方正密切关注并将全面评估美方相关举措，后续将视情适时决定调整针对美方原芬太尼关税和对等关税的反制措施。中方将保留采取一切必要措施的权利，坚决捍卫自身合法权益。中方一贯反对各种形式的单边关税措施，敦促美方取消并不再加征有关单边关税。中方愿与美方在将于近期举行的第6轮中美经贸磋商中开展坦诚磋商。

[Translation: Commerce Ministry spokesperson stated China is closely monitoring and will adjust countermeasures against original U.S. fentanyl and reciprocal tariffs. China reserves the right to take all necessary measures. China urges the U.S. to cancel unilateral tariffs. China is willing to engage in candid consultations in the upcoming 6th round of China-U.S. economic and trade talks.]
