## About the National Security Archive

The National Security Archive is an American 501(c)(3) non-profit research and archival institution located on the campus of the George Washington University. It publishes and digitizes declassified U.S. government documents, obtained through the Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) and other means, on a wide range of foreign policy and national security topics.

The Archive's Cuba Project, directed by Peter Kornbluh, has built the world's largest collection of declassified U.S. government records on Cuba, covering the Bay of Pigs invasion, the Cuban Missile Crisis, U.S.-Cuba back-channel negotiations, Operation Mongoose, the Brothers to the Rescue shootdown, and decades of covert operations against the Cuban government. Archive staff presented thousands of declassified documents directly to Fidel Castro in Havana in 1992 and 2002.
