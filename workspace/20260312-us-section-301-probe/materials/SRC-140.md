Published: 12 Feb 2026

Developing economies have seen their relative tariff disadvantage grow from around 1 to nearly 3 percentage points. Least developed countries, previously in a neutral position, now face an estimated disadvantage of about 2 percentage points. Developed economies appear less affected.

Tariff changes reinforce existing specialization and limit upgrading opportunities for developing countries (e.g., cocoa-chocolate value chain).
